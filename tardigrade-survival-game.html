The Trick

It senses catastrophe coming and pulls its legs in, expelling nearly all of its internal water. It curls into a tough, glassy shape scientists call a tun. In this desiccated, almost ametabolic state — a state called cryptobiosis — its biology simply pauses. Not quite alive. Not quite dead. Waiting. This is what makes it one of biology's most extreme extremophiles: built not to resist hardship, but to disappear from it entirely, until it's safe to come back.

Tardigrade Survival Mode

Explore. Eat. Survive 90 Seconds.

This is a whole world, not one room — move with WASD or the arrow keys, or click-and-hold (drag on touch), and the view scrolls with you (check the mini-map, top right). You already have all 5 survival powers — your job is just to switch on the right one at the right time. Little green bits of food are scattered everywhere; eat them or you'll starve. Each colored zone is labeled with the real scientific term for what it takes to survive there, and every zone has its own predator guarding it. You'll also run into wild rival tardigrades — each one is bigger and carries a number badge showing its power level, and a red glow means it's stronger than you (it will hunt you), while a green glow means you're stronger (it flees, and catching it is a free meal). Switching any power on boosts your own power level, so it's not just for the zones. Every 15–20 seconds, watch for a random hazard that surges across the entire world too.

  • All 5 powers are ready from the start — no searching, no waiting
  • Click a power icon, or press 1–5, to switch it on — press again (or pick another) to switch it off
  • Only one power can be on at a time, so turn it off once you're clear of danger
  • Rival tardigrades: bigger + red glow = stronger, avoid it. Green glow = weaker, eat it for a boost
  • Any active power boosts your own power level — check the 💪 Power readout
  • Eat the small green food dots to keep your Hunger meter up — starve and you start losing HP
  • Watch for the ⚠️ warning banner — a world-wide surge is coming and only one power stops it
  • Keep your HP above zero for the full 90 seconds to win
